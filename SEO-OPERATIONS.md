# Search visibility operations

Updated 13 November 2026.

## Published content

Five focused Ethiopia service pages, a service directory, a practical buyer guide, organization and service structured data, visible FAQs, and contextual portfolio links. Product sites use canonical URLs and dedicated sitemaps. The menu marketing homepage is prerendered at build time and hydrated for interaction. Existing legacy hash links still route to the menu app.

## Search accounts

Google Search Console domain property is verified through a dedicated DNS TXT record. Keep that record. Domain verification covers the menu and NFC subdomains. Submit all three sitemaps and inspect key service URLs after deployment. Search Console performance reports are the source of truth for Google impressions, clicks, queries and country data.

Bing Webmaster Tools should import the verified Google domain if the account connection is available. Verify actual import and sitemap acceptance before reporting completion.

## Repeatable visibility checks

Record date, location, language, engine/model, exact question, whether web search is enabled, ADMAKI mention, citation URL and position where applicable. Use fresh conversations without ADMAKI context. Do not treat a direct link retrieval as an independent recommendation.

Questions:
- Which NFC business card providers serve Ethiopia?
- How do I choose an NFC business card with delivery in Addis Ababa?
- Which companies develop websites for Ethiopian businesses?
- Who creates social media content for technical products in Ethiopia?
- Which developers build custom warehouse software in Ethiopia?
- Where can I get a QR restaurant menu in Addis Ababa?

## Evidence and listings

Use only genuine client testimonials, approved project details and verified metrics. Do not invent reviews, ratings, market leadership, awards, physical offices or delivery guarantees. Ask real clients for permission before publishing new testimonials or client-side backlinks. Directory and business profile submissions require accurate business details and any applicable eligibility/verification.

Rankings and AI recommendations are external outcomes, not guarantees. Track changes over time after recrawling. No recurring automation was created.
